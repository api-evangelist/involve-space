# Involve Space

Involve Space is an Italian stratospheric aerospace company building real-time Earth intelligence. It operates autonomous high-altitude balloon platforms (Stratostats) that loiter around 20 kilometers for hours, days, or weeks to deliver continuous, high-resolution Earth observation — bridging the gap between satellites, drones, and ground sensors. Its Neurostar AI flight software autonomously controls individual balloons and networks of them (LoonHive), while the CLEAR GeoAI platform turns captured imagery into geospatial insights for disaster response, infrastructure and environmental monitoring, security, and in-orbit-demonstration / payload validation.
